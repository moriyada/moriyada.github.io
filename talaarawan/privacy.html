Privacy notice

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Short version: Talaarawan doesn't know who you are, and we keep it that way. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number. You can delete your posts anytime.

This notice follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). It explains, in plain words, what we keep, why, and what you can do about it.

Who runs this

Talaarawan is a small personal project, part of Moriyada, run by one person (the site owner). The owner decides how the data here is used. Questions or requests: use Report on a post and pick "Something else", or contact the owner through the about page.

What we keep

  • A random anonymous ID. The first time you post, report or delete, your browser gets a random ID with no name, email or phone attached. It lets you delete your own posts and keeps the limits fair (like how many posts per hour). It is never shown on the site, not even to other writers.
  • Your posts: the text, the date and time, and whether they're public, waiting for review or hidden.
  • Safety notes about a post: what the automatic checks found (for example "harassment"), how many reports it got and the reasons people picked.
  • A scrambled version of your network (IP) address. We never store the address itself, only a one-way scrambled code, used for rate limits, to stop one person from reporting a post many times, and for temporary bans. It is erased from posts after 30 days, and from rate-limit counters after 2 days.
  • Refused posts are not saved. If a post is stopped (for example because it had a phone number), we only count that it happened and why. The text is not kept.

What we don't keep

  • No name, email, phone number, birthday, photo or location.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies, no ad networks, and we never sell anything about you.

What stays in your own browser

Your browser remembers your anonymous login, that you passed the age check and accepted the house rules, your unsent draft, and light/dark mode. Clearing your browser data erases these. Note: if you clear it, this browser forgets your anonymous ID, so you can't delete your old posts yourself anymore (see "How to delete" below).

Services that help run Talaarawan

  • Supabase stores the database and the anonymous accounts.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person and not a spam bot when you post or report. Cloudflare sees your IP address and basic browser details for this check.
  • OpenAI's moderation service reads the text of a post to check for hate, harassment, sexual content and threats. It isn't used to train their models, and only the post text is sent, never your ID.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files and, like any web host, sees your IP address when you visit.

Your posts are public

Anyone on the internet can read what you post, and someone could copy it. So please don't write anything that could identify you or someone else. The site blocks phone numbers, emails, addresses, social handles and full names together with schools or workplaces, but it can't catch everything.

If you write about hurting yourself

Your post is not blocked. You'll see the NCMH crisis hotline (1553 or 0917-899-8727), and the post is quietly flagged so the owner can look at it. The owner can't see who you are.

How to delete

  • On the Talaarawan page, under Your posts, tap Delete. The post is removed from the database right away, for good.
  • Lost access (new phone, cleared browser)? Find the post, tap Report and pick "It's mine and I can't delete it anymore". The owner will review it.

Posts stay until you or the owner delete them. The owner may hide or delete posts that break the house rules.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, and to file a complaint. Because we don't know who you are, we can only find "your" data through the anonymous ID in your browser. If you think your rights were violated, you can also contact the National Privacy Commission.

Age

You must be 20 or older to post.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
