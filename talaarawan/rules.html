House rules

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Talaarawan is a quiet place to write about your day. Para safe at masaya tayong lahat, here are the rules. They are also the terms for using the site.

1. Be kind.

Write about your life, your feelings, your day. Not about tearing other people down.

2. No doxxing. Keep everyone anonymous, including you.

  • Don't name real private people (classmates, exes, coworkers, neighbors). Use "a friend", "my boss", or initials.
  • No phone numbers, emails, home addresses, social media handles or links.
  • No full name together with a school or workplace.

3. No hate, harassment or threats.

No attacks on anyone for who they are (race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, where they're from), no bullying, no threats, no telling anyone to hurt themselves.

4. No sexual content.

Keep it clean. And never anything sexual involving minors; that gets removed and banned right away.

5. No spam, ads or scams.

No selling, no "follow me", no links to promote things. Posts are text only.

6. You must be 20 or older.

7. Hard feelings are welcome.

You can write about sadness, stress, grief or anger. If you're thinking about hurting yourself, please reach out: NCMH Crisis Hotline 1553 or 0917-899-8727, free and 24/7. In an emergency, call 911.

How rules are kept

  • Every post is checked automatically for personal info and for the things above. Some posts wait for a quick review before they show.
  • Anyone can Report a post. A post with 3 reports is hidden until the owner reviews it.
  • The owner may hide or delete posts that break these rules, and block anonymous accounts that keep breaking them.

Your words

What you write stays yours. By posting, you let Talaarawan show it publicly. You can delete it anytime from Your posts.

No guarantees

Talaarawan is a small free project. It may change, have downtime, or close. Posts reflect their writers, not the site owner. See the privacy notice for what's stored.
